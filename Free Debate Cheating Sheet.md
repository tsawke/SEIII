## Free Debate Cheating Sheet  
（社交媒体 16 岁以下禁令 · 自由辩论小抄）

---

### ARG 1：Cyberbullying – “More use, more abuse”

#### 1. 核心主张（Core Claim）

- **一句话概括：**  
  Social media has become the main stage for a global cyberbullying crisis; **the more teens use it, the more they are exposed to harm.**

- **重点记忆关键词：**  
  - “global cyberbullying crisis”  
  - “social media as the major venue”  
  - “more use → more risk → more harm”

---

#### 2. 关键数据（Key Stats to Drop）

- **全球流行率（Zhu et al., 2021, *Frontiers in Public Health*）**  
  - 14–58% of youth have **experienced cyberbullying**,  
  - 6–46% have **bullied others online**,  
  - Social media is a **major venue** for this abuse.  
  - 引用句式：  
    > A 2021 global review in *Frontiers in Public Health* found that between **14% and 58% of young people** have been cyberbullied, and up to **46% have bullied others**, mostly on social media.

- **风险与严重后果（Zhu 2021 + Grimland 2025 + Young 2024）**  
  - Cyberbullying is **consistently linked** to higher **depression, anxiety, and suicidal behavior**.  
  - CDC 2024 YRBS analysis（Young et al., 2024, *MMWR Supplements*）：
    - Frequent social media users ≈ **1.5×** the adjusted prevalence of **electronic bullying** compared to less frequent users.  
  - 引用句式：  
    > According to a 2024 CDC analysis of U.S. high school students, frequent social media users had about **1.5 times the rate of electronic bullying** compared with lighter users.

---

#### 3. 场上说法模板（How to Say It Smoothly）

- **铺垫 + 数据：**
  > We are not talking about a few isolated cases.  
  > A 2021 global review in *Frontiers in Public Health* shows that between **14% and 58% of young people** have been victims of cyberbullying, and up to **46% have bullied others**, mostly through social media platforms.

- **建立“多用 → 多伤害”的逻辑：**
  > And it’s not random. A 2024 CDC analysis published in *MMWR* found that **frequent social media users** had about **1.5 times the rate of electronic bullying** compared to less frequent users.  
  > In simple terms: **the more time teens spend on social media, the more likely they are to be bullied.**

- **连到心理危害：**
  > Reviews in *Frontiers in Public Health* and cross-national studies published by MDPI journals show that cyberbullying is strongly linked to **depression, anxiety, and even suicide attempts** among adolescents.

- **小结句：**
  > So when we allow under-16s unrestricted access to social media, we are not just giving them “connection”; we are **exposing them to a known bullying machine**.

---

#### 4. 数据来源可靠性（How to Defend Your Sources）

- **当对方问“你这些数据可靠吗？”：**
  - 回答要点：  
    - 来自 **peer-reviewed journals（同行评议期刊）**  
    - 有 **大样本 / 多国比较 / 政府官方数据**  
    - 公开发表，可查 DOI 与期刊名

- **可直接说的英文：**
  > These numbers are not from blogs or opinion pieces.  
  > They come from **peer-reviewed journals** like *Frontiers in Public Health* and **official CDC reports** in *MMWR*.  
  > That means the data has been checked by independent experts and based on **large, representative samples** of young people.

- **点名文献（方便记忆）：**
  - Zhu et al. (2021), *Frontiers in Public Health* – global review on cyberbullying.  
  - Young et al. (2024), *MMWR Supplements* – CDC national survey & social media frequency vs. electronic bullying.  
  - Grimland et al. (2025), *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health* – MDPI public-health journal, cross-national study on cyberbullying and suicide attempt.

### ARG 2：Physical Health – “A device that steals sleep and fuels obesity”

#### 1. 核心主张（Core Claim）

- **一句话概括：**  
  Early smartphone and social media use directly harms children’s **physical health**, especially **sleep** and **obesity**, creating long-term health risks.

- **关键词：**  
  - “early means riskier”  
  - “sleep and obesity risks”  
  - “body time bomb”

---

#### 2. 关键数据（Key Stats: Barzilay et al., 2025, *Pediatrics*）

- 样本：**10,000+ U.S. adolescents**  
- 在 12 岁时拥有智能手机 vs. 没有手机：  
  - ≈ **40% higher odds of obesity**  
  - ≈ **62% higher odds of insufficient sleep**  
- “Earlier means riskier”：  
  - 每提前一年拿到手机，**肥胖和睡眠不足风险增加约 8–9%**  
- 对于 13 岁：  
  - 在过去一年内刚拿到手机的孩子，**睡眠不足的风险约为无手机同龄人的 1.5 倍**，并且临床级心理病理风险也更高。

- **引用句：**
  > A 2025 study in *Pediatrics* following over **10,000 U.S. adolescents** found that 12-year-olds with smartphones had a **40% higher risk of obesity** and a **62% higher risk of insufficient sleep** compared with peers without phones.  
  > The earlier a child gets a phone, the higher the annual risk – about **8–9% more risk of obesity and sleep problems per year**.

---

#### 3. 场上说法模板（How to Phrase It）

- **先给画面感 + 数据：**
  > When we give a 12-year-old a smartphone with social media, we’re not just giving them a gadget.  
  > We are quietly raising their risk of **obesity and sleep deprivation**.  
  > A 2025 study in *Pediatrics* with over **10,000 adolescents** found that 12-year-olds with smartphones had **40% higher obesity risk** and **62% higher odds of insufficient sleep**.

- **强调“越早越危险”：**
  > The same study shows an “**earlier means riskier**” pattern:  
  > for **each year earlier** a child receives a smartphone, the risks of obesity and sleep problems rise by about **8–9% per year**.  
  > That is a **health deficit piling up** during critical years of growth.

- **连到长期身体后果：**
  > This isn’t just about feeling tired.  
  > Chronic sleep loss and obesity in early adolescence are risk factors for **diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and weakened immunity** later in life.  
  > In other words, we are planting a **body time bomb** when we allow under-16s unrestricted smartphone and social media use.

---

#### 4. 数据来源可靠性（Source Credibility）

- **回答要点：**
  - *Pediatrics* 是儿科领域的旗舰期刊之一（top pediatrics journal）。  
  - 文章有大样本（10,000+），采用统计控制（如年龄、性别、社会经济状态等）。  
  - 有 DOI，可追踪原文。

- **英文说法示例：**
  > This is not a small or biased study.  
  > It was published in *Pediatrics*, one of the leading medical journals for child health, using data from more than **10,000 adolescents**.  
  > The results are peer-reviewed and statistically controlled, which makes them **highly reliable** for policy debates.

- **记忆用文献名：**  
  - Barzilay et al. (2025), *Pediatrics* – smartphone ownership, age of acquisition & health outcomes.

### ARG 3：Brain & Social Development – “Digital brainrot and real-world social atrophy”

#### 1. 核心主张（Core Claim）

- **一句话概括：**  
  Early, heavy social media and smartphone use **reshapes adolescent brain development** and **erodes real-world social skills**, leading to long-term cognitive and social costs.

- **关键词：**  
  - “developing brain under construction”  
  - “hijacked reward system”  
  - “less attention, less empathy”

---

#### 2. 关键数据与结论（Key Evidence）

- **大脑发育：Maza et al. (2023), *JAMA Pediatrics***  
  - 研究对象：**169 early adolescents**，追踪约 3 年。  
  - **习惯性刷社交媒体的青少年**（经常检查平台）：
    - 在奖赏、情绪和自我控制相关脑区（如 ventral striatum, amygdala, prefrontal cortex）出现 **不同的发育轨迹**。  
    - 随时间推移，对社交奖赏/惩罚的神经敏感性 **越来越高**。  
  - 结论：早期习惯性检查社交媒体可能 **改变大脑对社交反馈的处理方式**。

- **问题性使用与心理负担：Shannon et al. (2022), *JMIR Mental Health***  
  - 系统综述 + 元分析（18 项研究，约 9300 人）：  
    - Problematic social media use 与 **抑郁、焦虑、压力** 均呈 **中度正相关**（r ≈ 0.27–0.35）。  
  - 说明：当使用成为强迫性或成瘾模式时，对心理功能的损伤相当显著。

- **青少年大脑脆弱性：Office of the Surgeon General (2023)**  
  - 报告强调青春期大脑的 **奖励系统过度敏感**，而自我控制相关前额叶尚未成熟。  
  - 警告：社交媒体平台使用强大的算法和奖励机制（点赞、推送）对青少年大脑可能造成 **尚未充分评估的长期影响**。

---

#### 3. 场上说法模板（Speaking Templates）

- **先解释“大脑在施工中”：**
  > The adolescent brain is **under construction**.  
  > The reward circuits are very sensitive, while the self-control centers in the prefrontal cortex are not fully developed yet.

- **引入 JAMA Pediatrics 的 fMRI 纵向研究：**
  > A 2023 brain-imaging study in *JAMA Pediatrics* followed **169 early adolescents** for about three years.  
  > It found that teens who **habitually checked social media** showed **increasing sensitivity** in brain regions involved in social rewards and punishment, while non-habitual users did not.  
  > In plain English: the more they repeatedly check social media, the more their brains are **wired to crave online feedback**.

- **连到“brainrot / 注意力受损”的概念：**
  > This constant training for quick rewards and notifications **undermines deep focus and self-control**.  
  > When this happens during a critical developmental window, it’s not just a bad habit—it is **a change in brain architecture**.

- **加入元分析：**
  > A 2022 meta-analysis in *JMIR Mental Health* shows that problematic social media use has **moderate correlations** with depression, anxiety, and stress.  
  > That means when use becomes compulsive, it **seriously damages mental functioning**, which is exactly what the developing brain relies on for learning.

- **刻画“社交萎缩”：**
  > At the same time, online interaction replaces face-to-face communication.  
  > The U.S. Surgeon General’s advisory warns that heavy social media use can **displace real-world relationships** and is linked with higher loneliness and distress.  
  > So we end up with teens who are constantly “connected” online but **less capable of reading emotions, resolving conflicts, and building deep relationships** offline.

- **总结句：**
  > In short, early, heavy smartphone and social media use acts like a **timed debilitator** for the developing mind and social skills—that’s why we must draw the line at 16.

---

#### 4. 数据来源可靠性（Source Credibility）

- **可用的英文话术：**
  > My evidence comes from **top-tier, peer-reviewed medical journals** and official health authorities.  
  > For example, *JAMA Pediatrics* and *JMIR Mental Health* are respected journals, and the U.S. Surgeon General’s advisory is a national public-health document.  
  > These are not opinions; they are based on **longitudinal brain scans, meta-analyses, and national surveys**.

- **记忆文献名：**
  - Maza et al. (2023), *JAMA Pediatrics* – habitual checking & brain development.  
  - Shannon et al. (2022), *JMIR Mental Health* – meta-analysis on problematic social media use & mental health.  
  - Office of the Surgeon General (2023) – official advisory on social media and youth mental health.

## Introduction Cheating Sheet  
（立论开场小抄）

> 目标：在 30–60 秒内完成 **问候 + 题目 + 立场 + 框架 + 三点 preview + 价值判断**。

---

### 1. 开头问候 & 点题（Greeting & Motion）

- 模板：
  > Good [morning/afternoon/evening], judges, opponents, and everyone here.  
  > Today we are debating a motion that will shape an entire generation:  
  > **“Social media should be banned for those under 16.”**

- 关键词：  
  - “shape an entire generation”  
  - “we are debating a motion that will shape…”

---

### 2. 明确立场（Stance）

- 模板：
  > As the Proposition, we say **yes** to this ban —  
  > not because we underestimate young people,  
  > but because we refuse to ignore what we are exposing them to.

- 记忆点：  
  - “not because we underestimate them, but because we refuse to ignore the risks.”

---

### 3. 框架：社交媒体不是中性工具（Framing）

- 模板：
  > For children, social media is **not a neutral tool**.  
  > It is a 24/7 environment designed to grab their attention,  
  > amplify comparison, and keep them scrolling long after their minds and bodies should be resting.  
  > When we give a 12- or 13-year-old a smartphone with unrestricted social media,  
  > we are not just giving them “access to the world”;  
  > we are **placing them inside an ecosystem** that can harm them emotionally, physically, and neurologically.

- 关键词：  
  - “not a neutral tool”  
  - “24/7 environment”  
  - “placing them inside an ecosystem”

---

### 4. 三大论点 Preview（Roadmap）

- 模板（对应你三个 ARG）：
  > In this debate, I will focus on **three core reasons** why a ban under 16 is necessary.  
  > **First**, social media is the main stage for a massive **cyberbullying crisis**, where more use means more harm.  
  > **Second**, early smartphone and social media use directly damages **physical health**, stealing sleep and fuelling obesity.  
  > **Third**, these platforms quietly drive **“digital brainrot” and real-world social atrophy**, reshaping the developing brain and weakening real-life social skills.

- 记忆点：  
  - “cyberbullying crisis”  
  - “steals sleep and fuels obesity”  
  - “digital brainrot and social atrophy”

---

### 5. 价值判断 + 重申动议（Value Statement & Close）

- 模板：
  > These are not minor side effects of “growing up online.”  
  > They are **preventable harms** created by an adult-designed system that children are not yet ready to manage.  
  > A temporary ban on social media for those under 16 is not a punishment;  
  > it is a **protective line** we draw so that children can develop healthy bodies, minds, and relationships  
  > before entering this powerful digital world.  
  > For these reasons, this House **should ban social media for those under 16**. Thank you.

- 关键词：  
  - “preventable harms”  
  - “protective line we draw”  
  - “before entering this powerful digital world”

---

  > and the CDC’s *MMWR* series, as well as the **U.S. Surgeon General’s advisory**.

  > These are not opinions or social media posts.  
  > They are based on **large, representative samples** and have been checked by independent experts,  
  > which makes them **reliable enough to guide public policy**, including this debate.

- **万能句 3：如果需要具体文献名**
  > For example, the numbers on obesity and sleep come from *Pediatrics* (Barzilay et al., 2025),  
  > the cyberbullying figures from *Frontiers in Public Health* (Zhu et al., 2021) and CDC’s *MMWR* (Young et al., 2024),  
  > and the brain-development evidence from *JAMA Pediatrics* (Maza et al., 2023).
